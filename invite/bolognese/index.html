Step by step

  1. 1Remove sleeve
  2. 2Pierce film
  3. 3Place on a suitable plate or baking trayMicrowave: a microwave-safe plate. Oven: a baking tray.
  4. 4Heat using the settings above
  5. 5Carefully peel off film
  6. 6Stand for 1 minute
  7. 7Stir & serve
  • The tray gets hot and may bend. Watch for steam when you peel off the film.
  • Make sure it's piping hot throughout. Do not reheat.

From the pack: all appliances vary and these are guidelines only. The pack suggests the microwave for best results.
